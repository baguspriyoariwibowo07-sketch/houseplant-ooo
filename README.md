A single out-of-office auto-reply written as if a houseplant sent it, written by `main.ts` to `/output/entry.txt` — nothing else.

## The line

> Out of office: on a performance plan for not growing. Direct all sunlight complaints to HR.

## Why this line

- **A real auto-reply:** it opens with "Out of office", gives a reason, and routes senders elsewhere — the form is exact.
- **Plant voice:** the plant's own life drives it — growing and sunlight are its whole job and its whole grievance.
- **Surprise:** office machinery (a performance plan, HR) applied to a plant for the one thing it cannot help.
- **Fresh:** the field saturates with "last watered in March" and indifferent cacti; a plant on a PIP is untouched.
- **Tight:** 91 characters against the 120-character gate, punch in the last three words.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts            # writes /output/entry.txt
OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
